Our Engineering Mission

Electricity billing operations across Pakistan DISCO sub-divisions handle millions of consumer transactions every billing cycle. Commercial assistants and computer operators across LESCO, GEPCO, FESCO, IESCO, MEPCO, PESCO, HAZECO, SEPCO, HESCO, QESCO, and TESCO frequently encounter mismatched bank collection scrolls, unposted consumer vouchers, and manual conversion hurdles.

DISCO Tools was engineered to solve these exact operational friction points across all electricity distribution companies in Pakistan. By automating CP-139 and CP-136 posting checks, converting locked PDF ledgers to formula-ready Excel files, and providing bulk bill scrapers with instant DISCO selection or custom URL configuration, we turn days of tedious audits into a 30-second automated run.
