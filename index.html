Our Engineering Mission

Electricity billing operations at PESCO sub-divisions handle millions of transactions across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Yet commercial assistants and computer operators frequently encounter mismatched bank collection scrolls, unposted consumer vouchers, and manual conversion hurdles.

PESCO Tools was engineered to solve these exact operational friction points. By automating CP-139 and CP-136 posting checks, converting locked PDF ledgers to formula-ready Excel files, and providing bulk scrapers, we turn days of tedious audits into a 30-second automated run.
